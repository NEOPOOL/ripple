# Privacy Policy for Ripple

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026  
**Publisher:** Vayu Labs (`com.vayulabs.ripple`)  
**Contact:** `meetvayu@gmail.com`

---

### 1. Overview
At **Vayu Labs**, we believe that mental health, anxiety relief, and somatic grounding tools must be private by default. **Ripple** was architected from the ground up as a **100% offline-first application**. 

We do not believe in monetizing attention, tracking users in distress, or harvesting biometric and somatic habits.

---

### 2. Information We Collect
**We collect ZERO personal data.**
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
- **No Location Data:** We do not track GPS, IP geolocation, or cell tower signals.
- **No Usage Analytics or Telemetry:** There are no third-party tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer) embedded in Ripple.
- **No Health or Biometric Records:** Your breathing session intervals, somatic interaction choices, and thought dissolver entries are stored strictly in volatile device memory or private local app sandbox preferences (`EncryptedSharedPreferences` / DataStore).

---

### 3. On-Device Processing
All features within Ripple operate entirely on your device hardware without requiring an internet connection:
- **Procedural Audio Synthesizers:** Soundscapes, binaural beats, singing bowl resonances, and sand raking friction are procedurally synthesized in real time via on-device audio buffers (`AudioTrack` / `SoundPool`). No sound files are streamed from remote servers.
- **Thought Dissolver:** Any text typed into the thought dissolver is animated into fading particles entirely within local GPU memory and immediately destroyed. Nothing is ever logged or transmitted over a network.
- **Haptic Engine:** All tactile feedback waveforms are driven directly by your device's vibration actuator.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
Ripple does **not** integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or behavioral trackers. 

---

### 5. Permissions
Ripple requests only the minimal hardware permissions required for its tactile features:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Required to generate tactile micro-vibrations for the worry stone, bubbles, sand rake, and breathing pacers.
- `android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`: Required to maintain smooth, uninterrupted audio synthesis during long sleep-timer sessions when the screen is dimmed.
- `android.permission.BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE`: Required solely to enable the "Instant Calm" Quick Settings tile in your Android notification shade.

---

### 6. Medical & Wellness Disclaimer
Ripple is intended solely as an educational self-regulation and somatic relaxation tool. It is **not** a medical device, nor does it diagnose, cure, treat, or prevent clinical anxiety disorders, panic disorders, depression, or psychiatric emergencies. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact your local healthcare provider or emergency services immediately:
- **US & Canada:** Call or text 988
- **United Kingdom:** Call 111 or text SHOUT to 85258
- **India:** Tele-MANAS (14416)
- **International:** 112

---

Ripple does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
