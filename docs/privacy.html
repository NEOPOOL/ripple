Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026 • Published by Vayu Labs (com.vayulabs.ripple)
Core Privacy Commitment: Ripple was architected as a 100% offline-first application. We collect zero personal data, track zero analytics, embed zero advertisements, and operate zero remote telemetry.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information.

  • No Identifiers: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or user IDs.
  • No Location Data: We do not request or track GPS, IP geolocation, or Wi-Fi data.
  • No Usage Telemetry: There are no third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer) embedded in Ripple.
  • No Health Records: Breathing session counts, tactile choices, and thought dissolver entries are stored strictly in local device sandbox memory.

2. On-Device Processing

Every feature within Ripple calculates locally on your device hardware without requiring an internet connection:

  • Procedural Audio: Soundscapes, binaural beats, and tactile scraping frequencies are generated in real-time via local PCM buffers. No sound files are streamed from external servers.
  • Thought Dissolver: Thoughts typed into the dissolver are converted into particle meshes within GPU memory and destroyed immediately upon dissolution. Nothing is ever written to disk or transmitted over a network.
  • Haptic Synthesis: Tactile waveforms are triggered directly through your phone's vibration actuator.

3. Permissions Requested

Ripple requests only the minimal hardware permissions required to deliver somatic feedback:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Needed to drive the tactile micro-vibration sensations for the worry stone, bubbles, sand rake, and breath pacers.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Keeps procedural audio streaming smoothly during sleep-timer soundscape sessions when the screen dims.
  • android.permission.BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE: Allows optional single-tap launch from your Android Quick Settings notification shade.

4. Medical & Wellness Disclaimer

Ripple is an educational self-regulation and somatic relaxation companion. It is not a medical device, nor does it diagnose, cure, treat, or prevent clinical anxiety disorders, panic disorders, depression, or psychiatric emergencies. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact your local healthcare provider or emergency services immediately:

  • US & Canada: Dial or text 988
  • United Kingdom: Dial 111 or text SHOUT to 85258
  • India: Tele-MANAS (14416)
  • International: Dial 112
